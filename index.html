Our UX Design Project

By Dominic & Stephen

Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding user needs and improving design through research methods such as affinity diagrams and personas. Our goal is to create a user-centered solution based on real insights.

Affinity Diagram

Our group worked together to identify key ideas and organize them into themes. This helped us better understand patterns and guide our design decisions.

Persona

I interviewed some people to gather their opinions on our Calorie Tracking App and to understand what types of users would benefit from it.
